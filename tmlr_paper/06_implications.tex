\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Implications for Mechanistic Interpretability}

Our findings have several implications for the field:

\paragraph{Superposition is fundamental, not artifactual.} The persistence of superposition across toy autoencoders, transformers, and translation models suggests it is a general strategy neural networks employ to manage limited capacity, not an artifact of simplified experimental settings.

\paragraph{Sparsity drives superposition.} In all architectures, increased sparsity leads to more superposition. This aligns with the theoretical prediction that interference costs decrease when features are rarely co-active.

\paragraph{Practical models use superposition.} The translation model's ability to maintain performance through a 32$\times$ compression (512$\rightarrow$16 dimensions) demonstrates that superposition enables efficient encoding of real linguistic features, not just synthetic ones.

\subsection{Implications for AI Safety}

Understanding superposition is crucial for AI safety efforts. First, superposition complicates the identification of individual features; techniques like sparse autoencoders \citep{bricken2023monosemanticity} aim to disentangle superposed representations. Second, \citet{templeton2024scaling} showed that identified features can be used to steer model behavior, and our results suggest this approach should generalize across architectures. Third, superposition may contribute to unexpected model behaviors when rarely-co-occurring features interfere; understanding superposition patterns could help predict such failures.

\subsection{Implications for Architecture Design}

Our results suggest several opportunities for architecture design. Architectures that reduce superposition (e.g., wider layers, SoLU activations) may be more interpretable, though potentially at efficiency cost. The success of our translation bottleneck suggests that controlled capacity constraints could induce efficient, structured representations---potentially useful for model compression. Finally, the distinction between representations with and without natural basis alignment \citep{hubinger2023privileged} affects how superposition manifests and should inform interpretation strategies.
